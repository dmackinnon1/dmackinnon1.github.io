Safe Harbour

Place a piece in each region such that every region has one piece and no pieces can capture each other.

Click on the piece selection button to choose the piece type used for the puzzle. Piece selection will generate a new puzzle based on that type.

Generally, king, knight, and rook puzzles are easiest, while bishop and queen puzzles can be more challenging.
