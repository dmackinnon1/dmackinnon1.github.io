Tour Generator


Using this page, you can explore how new possible paths are opened up by hiding the numbers on specific squares. When a value on a square is hidden, alternate paths that preserve the remaining exposed numbers will be calculated. You cannot hide the first square (square numbered 1), but you can hide any other cells. If you hide many cells, the calculations to find alternate paths may start to take longer. The number of alternate paths varies depending on the chess piece type that you choose. The piece represented by the circled "X" is a wazir, the piece that Numbrix puzzles follow: it moves one square up, down, left, or right. The asterisk (*) represents a piece that can move to any square - the graph of its available moves is the complete graph (K64). With this piece, hiding even a few squares allows a great many paths, so the page stops counting at 1,000.
