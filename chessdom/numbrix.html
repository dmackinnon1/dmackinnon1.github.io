Numbrix Puzzles

The puzzles on this page present a variation of Numbrix-style puzzles, originally created by Marilyn vos Savant. Like other path puzzles (such as Kixote and Hidato), your goal is to trace a path over the whole board, touching each cell only once. In Numbrix puzzles, you are restricted to moving only up, down, left, and right - as in a Hidato puzzle without the diagonal moves.
